## Responsibilities

The Communications Committee (CC) should have a main organisational role for managing the website, almost like a project manager role that makes sure the other committees have the access they need, and update the site. 

Each Committee should be responsible for updating and maintaining their own content. Each Committee should assign people who are responsible for this, or integrate website maintenace into their tasks however they see fit. Optionally, Committees can request an update and send the CC the content to be updated (Example, send in event information and we update the site).
